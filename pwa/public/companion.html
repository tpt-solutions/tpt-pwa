tpt-cortex companion

The optional Android companion that runs your cortex daemon — true background sync for the PWA, no Play Store required.

  1. Install the PWA in your browser first (open the app, use your browser's Install option).
  2. Download the APK from the latest release and open it.
  3. Allow your browser to install apps when Android asks (direct distribution, signed builds only).
  4. Open the companion once — it starts the daemon as a persistent service and bridges the PWA to native background sync.
Download latest APK

The companion only ever talks to ws://127.0.0.1:9911 on your device. Without it, the PWA keeps working fully offline via Service Worker + local storage.
